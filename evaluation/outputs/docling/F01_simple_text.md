## Introduction to Document Analysis

Document analysis is a foundational discipline within modern information retrieval and knowledge engineering.  It  encompasses  the  systematic  extraction  of  structured  and  unstructured  information from diverse digital document formats. Modern document pipelines require resilient parsers capable of interpreting typographical conventions and semantic hierarchies.

## Core Concepts and Principles

The primary challenge in document conversion lies in bridging visual presentation with machine-actionable structure. Visual documents such as PDFs encode absolute glyph placements rather than semantic document objects such as headings, paragraphs, and list items.

A  robust  converter  must  accurately  infer  the  underlying  document  graph  without  hallucinating non-existent formatting or omitting critical textual passages.
